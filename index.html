Questions people ask

What does it actually do?

It runs your job search with you: finds roles, finds the people inside who can refer you, writes and sends personal outreach, follows up, tailors your resume for application systems, applies, and tracks it all in one dashboard.

Does it send messages on its own?

Only if you let it. Switch to Review mode and nothing goes out until you approve it. You can change this any time in Settings.

Where does my data live?

On your computer. Your resume, profile, drafts and history are never stored on our servers. Outreach passes through our relay for delivery and is not kept. Drafting uses your own AI model key, so prompts go to the provider you choose. Full security details.

Will my outreach read as AI slop?

No form letters. Each note is short, written for one person, and built only from what's actually in your resume. It's checked before it goes out so nothing is invented, nobody gets the same message twice, and in Review mode you approve every word.

How is it kept secure?

Both apps are signed by a verified developer: Apple Developer ID on Mac and Microsoft Trusted Signing on Windows. Your keys sit in your computer's Keychain or Credential Manager, the dashboard only opens on your own computer, and updates install only when you approve them. See how Your Sidedoor protects you.

What do I need?

A Mac or Windows computer, about 15 minutes, and the accounts the setup guide walks you through, including your own AI model key.

How much does it cost?

14 days free, then $37 a month if you keep it. Cancel any time from Settings, Manage subscription, in the app; first-month refunds within 14 days, no questions.

No tool can. It gets you in front of the right people faster; the interviews are yours to win.
