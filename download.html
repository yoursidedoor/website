Which Mac do I have?

Click the Apple menu in the top-left corner of your screen, then click About This Mac.

  • If it says Chip: Apple M… (for example M1, M2 or M3), you have an Apple chip.
  • If it says Processor: Intel…, you have an Intel chip.
