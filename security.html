Security, in plain words.

Your Sidedoor writes to people in your name, so it should be easy to see exactly what it does with your data. Everything on this page describes how the software works today, and the source code is public so you can check it.

The short version

Your data lives on your computer

Your resume, profile, drafts, message history and applications are stored in a folder on your own computer. There is no Sidedoor account and no cloud copy of your search.

Your keys are in your computer's vault

Your AI key and license key are kept in macOS Keychain or Windows Credential Manager, not in plain files, so they stay out of backups and synced folders.

We don't keep your messages

Outreach passes through our relay on its way out and is not stored.

We never see your drafts being written

Drafting uses your own AI key, so your resume and drafts go straight from your computer to the AI provider you choose, not to us.

How it's protected

A dashboard only your computer can open

The dashboard opens only on your own computer, never on your network, and a web page you visit can't reach it.

Each account is walled off

One user can't read or send through anyone else's account.

Encrypted in transit

Everything Sidedoor sends over the internet is encrypted.

No tracking inside the app

The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting services. Our website counts visits without cookies.

You choose how much it does on its own

In Review mode, no message goes out until you approve it. You can switch modes any time in Settings.

Open code

The full source is public on GitHub under the Business Source License, so anyone can read exactly what it does.

Signed by a verified developer

Both apps are signed by a verified developer and checked by Apple and Microsoft. If anyone altered the app, your computer would warn you. Sidedoor never updates itself without asking.
