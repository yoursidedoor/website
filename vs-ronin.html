The short version

Which one fits you

Ronin is likely the better fit if
  • You already know people at most of the companies you want.
  • You'd rather submit every application yourself.
  • You want a resume and cover letter rewritten for each posting.
  • You don't want to set up your own AI model key.
Your Sidedoor is likely the better fit if
  • You don't know anyone yet at the companies you want.
  • You want the research and the first hello done every day without you.
  • You want your data to stay on your own computer.
  • You'd rather not count drafts against a monthly cap.

Questions people ask

Will my outreach read as AI slop?

No form letters. Each note is short, written for one person, and built only from what's actually in your resume. It's checked before it goes out so nothing is invented, nobody gets the same message twice, and in Review mode you approve every word.

Where do the jobs come from, and how fresh are they?

From the employers themselves: company careers pages and the application systems they post to. A listing on another site counts only if the employer posted it there in the last 30 days, and staffing agencies and scraped aggregators are used only to double-check. Applications always go to the employer's own page.

Why pay $36 when there are cheaper tools?

Cheaper tools mostly send applications into the pile. This one does the part they skip: finding the people inside each company who can refer you and writing to each of them personally. It's 14 days free with no card needed, then $36 a month with no tiers, and you bring your own AI model key. First-month refunds within 14 days, no questions.

Does it guarantee a job?

No tool can. It gets you in front of the right people faster; the interviews are yours to win.
